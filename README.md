# Awesome-Educational-Math-Solver



**Curated List of Commercial Apps & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Step-by-Step Solving, Graphing, Computer Algebra Systems & Math Education*

**Last updated: October 2026**



This repository tracks notable **commercial math solvers** and **open-source projects** for **Educational Math Solving**. These tools help students, teachers, and self-learners solve equations, check homework, visualize functions, and understand mathematical concepts step by step.



**Examples** include Microsoft Math Solver, Photomath, Symbolab, Wolfram Alpha, Cymath, Mathway, QuickMath, Socratic, Maple Calculator, and GeoGebra (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: The open-source math software ecosystem is **exceptionally mature and production-proven**. **GeoGebra** leads interactive geometry and algebra learning with cross-platform support and educational awards . **Maxima** and **SageMath** provide full computer algebra system (CAS) capabilities used in university education . **microMathematics Plus** is a 100% open-source Android worksheet-based graphing calculator with maximum privacy—no ads, no trackers, no telemetry . **Mathomatic** is a small, portable symbolic math program that can generate optimized C, Java, and Python code from simplified equations .

## ☁️ SaaS/Hosted Platforms



> **📊 Market Context**: The global educational math solver market is estimated at **~$2.5B in 2026**, growing toward **~$6B by 2032**. The sector is **moderately fragmented** — **Microsoft Math Solver** offers **fully free step-by-step solutions with no paywall** on its steps , while **Photomath Plus** charges **$9.99/month or $69.99/year** for animated tutorials . **Symbolab Pro** costs **$9.99/month or $39.99/year** for unlimited step counts . **Wolfram Alpha** gates its best step-by-step explanations behind a **Pro subscription** . **Mathway** and **Cymath** offer free tiers with limited daily steps . No single vendor holds a winner-take-all position; students typically mix free and paid tools based on the depth of explanation needed.

## ⚠️ Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Math solver apps handle potentially sensitive student data; review privacy policies before classroom or institutional use.

- **Free tier caveats**: **Microsoft Math Solver is genuinely free with no paywalled steps** . **Photomath's animated explanations require Photomath Plus** ($9.99/month or $69.99/year) . **Symbolab's free tier limits daily steps**; Pro is $9.99/month or $39.99/year . **Wolfram Alpha gates full step-by-step behind Pro** . **Mathway limits free daily steps** .

- **Open-source reality**: The open-source ecosystem for educational math solving is **exceptionally mature and production-proven**. **GeoGebra** is free, multi-platform, and award-winning for interactive math learning . **Maxima** and **SageMath** provide full CAS capabilities for university-level work . **microMathematics Plus** is a 100% open-source Android worksheet calculator with maximum privacy—no ads, no trackers, no telemetry . **Mathomatic** generates optimized code from simplified equations . However, **commercial apps** (Photomath, Symbolab, Wolfram Alpha) provide **polished camera input, animated tutorials, and massive problem databases** that open-source alternatives may lack. The open-source path is **genuinely viable** for students, teachers, and anyone seeking privacy-respecting math tools.

- **Privacy note**: **microMathematics Plus** explicitly states "maximum privacy: no ads, no trackers, no telemetry, no special permissions" . This makes it ideal for privacy-conscious students and educational institutions with strict data policies.
